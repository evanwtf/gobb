What a lock does

A locked forum takes no new topics and no replies. Members who hold the moderator right mod.lock in that forum can still post there. A lock does not hide the forum, stop reading, or stop edits and deletes. It does not reach the subforums: lock each one on its own. A category or a link holds no topics, so it cannot be locked.
